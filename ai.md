- **Definition**: Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science dedicated to creating systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as perception, reasoning, learning, and decision‑making.

- **Historical milestones**
  - 1950: Alan Turing proposes the Turing Test.
  - 1956: Dartmouth Workshop coins the term “Artificial Intelligence.”
  - 1970s–80s: Expert systems (e.g., MYCIN) demonstrate rule‑based reasoning.
  - 1997: IBM Deep Blue defeats world chess champion Garry Kasparov.
  - 2012: Deep learning breakthrough with AlexNet wins ImageNet competition.
  - 2018–2023: Large language models (GPT‑3, GPT‑4, PaLM, LLaMA) achieve human‑level language generation.

- **Core subfields**
  - Machine Learning (ML): algorithms that improve from data.
  - Deep Learning: neural networks with many layers.
  - Natural Language Processing (NLP).
  - Computer Vision.
  - Robotics.
  - Knowledge Representation & Reasoning.
  - AI Safety & Ethics.

- **Key algorithms & models**
  - Linear/Logistic regression, decision trees, SVMs, k‑NN.
  - Neural architectures: CNNs, RNNs/LSTMs, Transformers.
  - Reinforcement learning: Q‑learning, policy gradients, DQNs.
  - Probabilistic models: Bayesian networks, MDPs.
  - Generative models: GANs, VAEs, diffusion models.

- **Major applications**
  - Healthcare: diagnosis, drug discovery, personalized treatment.
  - Finance: fraud detection, algorithmic trading, credit scoring.
  - Transportation: autonomous vehicles, traffic optimization.
  - Customer service: chatbots, sentiment analysis, recommendation systems.
  - Manufacturing: predictive maintenance, quality inspection, supply‑chain optimization.
  - Science: protein folding (AlphaFold), climate modeling, materials discovery.

- **Ethical, legal, and societal concerns**
  - Bias and discrimination.
  - Transparency & explainability.
  - Data privacy.
  - Job displacement vs. augmentation.
  - Dual‑use risk (disinformation, deepfakes, autonomous weapons).
  - Regulatory efforts: EU AI Act, US Executive Orders, ISO/IEC AI standards.

- **Performance benchmarks**
  - ImageNet (state‑of‑the‑art top‑1 ~88%).
  - GLUE / SuperGLUE (GPT‑4 > 90%).
  - Atari 2600 (human‑level with DQN).
  - AlphaGo/AlphaZero: superhuman across Go, chess, shogi.

- **Current research frontiers**
  - Foundation models: scaling laws, emergent abilities, multimodal integration.
  - Efficient AI: model compression, sparsity, neuromorphic hardware.
  - Robustness: adversarial defenses, OOD detection.
  - Alignment: value learning, safe exploration.
  - Neurosymbolic AI.
  - Continual / lifelong learning.

- **Future outlook (2025‑2035)**
  - Domain‑specific foundation models (legal, medical).
  - Expanded regulatory frameworks.
  - Progress toward general‑purpose agents.
  - Emphasis on energy‑efficient AI.
  - AI‑augmented scientific discovery accelerating breakthroughs.
